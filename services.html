Generative AI and Microsoft 365 Copilot Training

A practical programme for teams who want to use AI assistants confidently in their daily work. Participants learn how to write effective prompts, check AI output, and apply Copilot or Gemini inside the tools they already use.

Every session includes a structured prompt engineering framework and exercises built on realistic tasks from your workplace.

What participants will be able to do

  • Draft emails, reports and proposals faster with Copilot in Word and Outlook
  • Analyse data and build summaries with Copilot in Excel

  • Check AI output for accuracy and use AI responsibly at work

AI agents can research, draft, summarise and take action across several steps. This programme shows teams how to design agents for real business tasks and how to keep people in control of the results.
