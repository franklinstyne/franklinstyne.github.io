Common questions

HRDCorp claims explained.

Who can claim HRDCorp funding?

Employers registered with HRDCorp who pay the HRD levy can use their levy balance to fund approved training for their employees, subject to HRDCorp's scheme rules.

Do we need to apply before the training?

Yes. The grant application should be submitted and approved through the HRDCorp system before the training takes place. We recommend starting the process as early as possible.

How much of the cost can we claim?

The claimable amount depends on your levy balance and the applicable HRDCorp scheme and cost limits. We will share the details you need to check this when we send the quotation.

Can the programme be customised and still be claimable?

Yes, in most cases. Talk to us about your requirements and we will advise on the best way to structure the programme for your application.

Can you train staff from organisations outside Malaysia?

Yes. We deliver across Malaysia, Brunei and India. HRDCorp funding applies to eligible Malaysian employers only.

HRDCorp rules and limits are updated from time to time. For the latest requirements, refer to hrdcorp.gov.my or speak to your HRDCorp officer.
